About Choco Port

A port of sweetness in Baku.

The Story

Where flavor meets atmosphere.

Choco Port brings together chocolate, coffee and dessert in one warm, considered space in Baku. It's a place built for the small rituals — a coffee before work, a dessert shared after dinner, a quiet corner for a long conversation.

Every detail, from the plating to the lighting, is chosen to make time slow down a little.

Our Philosophy

Simple ingredients.
Careful craft.
Warm hospitality.
